Questions

Before you visit

Not covered here? Call the clinic on +91 94972 88299.

How do I book an appointment?

Book online on Practo, which shows Dr. Shuhaib's available slots and confirms instantly. You can also call the clinic during opening hours.

What is the consultation fee?

₹700 for a consultation with Dr. Shuhaib. Treatment and procedure costs depend on the plan and are explained after the consultation.

Do I need a consultation before a procedure?

Yes. Treatments like peels, laser, PRP and GFC are only suitable for some skin types and conditions, so the doctor examines you first and recommends what fits.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Any previous prescriptions or reports, the names of creams and medicines you currently use, and, for a skin concern, come without makeup on the affected area if you can.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the treatment and on you. Laser hair reduction, peels, PRP and GFC are usually done as a course of sessions spaced weeks apart. The doctor will give you an estimate at the consultation.

Is there downtime after treatment?

Most facials and peels allow you to return to normal activity the same day, though some peels cause mild redness or peeling for a few days. For procedures like FUE, the doctor will explain recovery and aftercare in advance.
